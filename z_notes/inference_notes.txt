Inference layers:
a) Inference backend — --inference.type=sync|rtc

sync (default): one policy call per control tick.
rtc: Real-Time Chunking, for slow VLAs (Pi0, SmolVLA) where inference can't keep up with 30 Hz. Takes --inference.rtc.execution_horizon, --inference.rtc.max_guidance_weight. Overkill for a 2-layer MLP.
b) Rollout strategy — --strategy.type=base|sentry|highlight|dagger|episodic

base — run the policy, record nothing. What you want.
sentry — continuous recording + periodic upload.
highlight — ring buffer, press s to save the last N seconds.
dagger — human-in-the-loop; teleop leader can take over and record corrections.
episodic — episode-oriented recording with reset phases, like lerobot-record.
All but base require --dataset.repo_id.

c) Which policy — swap --policy.path for any checkpoint (ACT, Diffusion, Pi0, SmolVLA, or another of your own). --policy.discover_packages_path is only needed for out-of-tree plugins like this one.
